Shrimp and Grits

Shrimp and grits is a classic comfort dish that combines creamy grits with seasoned shrimp. This recipe walks you through the ingredients and steps needed to prepare a simple and delicious version of the meal.

Ingredients

  • Shrimp
  • Grits
  • Butter
  • Cheese
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Steps

  1. Prepare the grits according to the package instructions. Add butter and cheese to make them creamy and smooth.

  2. Season the shrimp with salt and pepper. Cook the shrimp in a pan until they are fully cooked and lightly browned.

  3. Combine the shrimp with the grits and mix gently. Serve while hot.
